## Full description — paste into console (plain text)

```
Tic-tac-toe for Android: get n in a row before your opponent. Play solo vs the computer, two players on one device, or with a friend on two phones over Bluetooth.

Bluetooth multiplayer
Host or join a match with someone nearby. Moves stay in sync. Only Bluetooth is used for the link — no online servers, no account, and no location access. Bluetooth permission is needed only to find and connect to your opponent.

Game modes
• vs computer — Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert (unbeatable on 3×3);
• two players on one device;
• series mode — first to 2, 3, 4, or 6 wins.

Customize the match: boards from 3×3 to 6×6 and win-line length from 3 to 6.

Themes, progress, and extras
• board themes Classic, Aurora, Grove, Ember, and Ocean — unlock new palettes by winning vs AI;
• vs-AI stats and two-player tallies, plus local achievements on the Scores tab;
• undo and hints, a home-screen widget, and share a finished round;
• first-launch onboarding, in-app language (System / English / Русский), and sound with a volume slider.

No ads and no sign-up. Progress stays on your device. Everyday play needs no network — Bluetooth is only for multiplayer.

Download and play a round now — solo or with a friend nearby.
```
