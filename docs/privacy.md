# Privacy Policy — Tic-tac-toe (Android)

**Last updated:** June 2026  
**Developer:** Aleksey Karakuts — [aleksey@karakuts.com](mailto:aleksey@karakuts.com)

## Summary

This app does **not** collect, transmit, or sell personal data. All game progress (statistics, settings, unlocked themes) is stored **only on your device**.

## Data stored locally

- Wins, losses, draws, and streaks against the computer
- Board size, win line length, opponent mode, and AI difficulty
- Selected theme and sound preference
- In-progress game state (restored after process death when possible)

Data is saved in app-private storage (`SharedPreferences`). It may be included in your device's **automatic backup** to your Google account if you have Android backup enabled.

## Data we do not collect

- No account registration
- No analytics or advertising SDKs
- No location, contacts, or microphone/camera access
- No network calls during normal gameplay

## Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties. Opening **Contact support** or **Privacy policy** in the About screen uses your browser or email client — only what you choose to send.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always in the project repository at this path.
